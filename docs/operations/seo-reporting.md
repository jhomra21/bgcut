# SEO reporting

Use one short weekly report and one deeper monthly review. Keep observed data separate from estimates.

## Weekly report

Report the period and the data source for every metric.

### Search Console

When Search Console is connected, include:

- total Google Search clicks and impressions;
- overall CTR and average position;
- top gaining queries by clicks or impressions;
- top losing queries;
- queries with meaningful impressions and average position from 5 to 20;
- pages receiving a query that belongs to another page in the keyword map;
- new indexing or canonical issues;
- new pages that have begun receiving impressions.

Do not call a rank change meaningful when the query has too little impression data to support it.

### Site changes

List only changes shipped during the week:

- new or materially revised indexable pages;
- title, description, canonical, internal-link, or schema changes;
- new tools;
- claim corrections;
- broken-link or redirect fixes.

### Competitors

List meaningful changes from the competitor watch:

- new pages targeting watched clusters;
- product, API, pricing, or deployment changes that affect a comparison;
- new directories or roundups worth evaluating;
- new benchmark claims that are reproducible enough to investigate.

### Distribution

Record:

- directory submissions made;
- outreach pitches sent;
- replies;
- links earned;
- social posts published.

### Next actions

Choose at most five actions. Prefer:

1. a page already earning impressions around positions 5 to 20;
2. a factual correction;
3. an indexing failure;
4. a query landing on the wrong page;
5. a new high-intent query not covered by the existing map.

Do not default to publishing another article.

## Monthly review

Use the monthly review to change strategy, not to repeat weekly metrics.

Review:

- query clusters gaining or losing demand;
- pages with sustained movement;
- whether comparison pages receive qualified traffic;
- whether free tools earn links or repeat visits;
- branded search and entity consistency;
- AI-answer errors or citations recorded during the month;
- competitor pages that gained estimated visibility from a named provider;
- outreach hit rate;
- content that should be consolidated, redirected, refreshed, or removed.

Update the keyword map when observed search behavior shows that a different page should own an intent.
